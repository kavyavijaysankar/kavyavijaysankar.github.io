Booking info Booking Fee (Deposit)
A 25% deposit is required to secure the booking. This covers the initial cost of renting equipment, set up, etc. If the shoot is cancelled up to 48 hours before the scheduled date, the deposit will be refunded. The remaining balance must be paid in full on the day of the shoot. The photos will not be sent until the full amount is received.

Rescheduling
For outdoor shoots, if the weather is too poor to shoot safely, we will reschedule at no extra cost. For general client reschedules, please give at least 48 hours' notice. Cancellations or reschedules made after this window will result in the loss of your deposit. A new 25% deposit will be required to book another date.

Studio Shoots
If you prefer an indoor setting, you can cover the rental cost of your chosen space and equipment. The photoshoot package price remains the same.

Files
Photos are delivered as high-quality JPEGs. RAW files are not included or available for purchase.

Turnaround Time
Your final edited gallery will be delivered within 2 weeks of the photoshoot date (can be up to 3 weeks for larger projects).

Access
Your photos will be delivered via a digital download link (typically Google Drive) which will remain active for 2 weeks. It is your responsibility to download and save your images within this timeframe. The photographer is not liable for files lost or deleted after the 2-week period expires.

Additional Images
Extra charges will be applied for any additional edited images outside of your package limit. These will be edited and delivered as high-quality JPEGs.
