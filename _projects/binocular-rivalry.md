This project reproduced the dynamical systems model of binocular rivalry from Wilson (2007), using coupled ordinary differential equations to model neural activity, adaptation, and perceptual switching. I used Python and XPPAUT for numerical simulation, parameter analysis, phase-plane analysis, eigenvalue-based stability analysis, and Hopf bifurcation analysis, including simulations of rivalry dynamics and perceptual memory.

I presented the project as a research poster, communicating the model, dynamical systems analysis, simulation results, and their implications for binocular rivalry and perceptual memory.
